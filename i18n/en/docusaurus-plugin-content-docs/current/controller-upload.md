# Upload a controller layout

FCL lets you build your own touch controller layouts and share them with everyone. This guide explains how to submit a layout and what the review group checks before it appears in the launcher.

:::caution Before you upload
Do not upload layouts made only for personal use. Only submit layouts that are designed for other players.
:::

## Upload steps

### 1. Open the controller page

Open FCL, click the controller icon in the left sidebar, then select the layout you want to upload. Press **Upload layout** — not **Share layout**.

![Select the layout and press Upload layout](/img/docs/upload-controller/upload.png)

### 2. Press Share

On the next screen press **Share**. This requires that you have already been admitted to the FCL controller review group.

![Press Share](/img/docs/upload-controller/share_pri.png)

### 3. Fill in the layout information

In the dialog, complete the layout information and choose the layout language and the device type it is designed for. Pick an icon and a screenshot with the buttons marked in red.

![Edit the layout information](/img/docs/upload-controller/info.png)

:::note
If you cannot find the image path, use a file manager (for example MT Manager) to move the image somewhere easy to find.
:::

### 4. Share the generated archive

Press **Share** again. FCL generates a compressed archive named with an eight-character ID. Send that archive to the review group and wait for a reviewer.

![The generated archive](/img/docs/upload-controller/share_sec.png)

![Sharing in the review group](/img/docs/upload-controller/community.png)

## Review rules

### Functionality and design

1. **Complete functionality** — the layout must provide the core controls.
2. **Originality** — do not copy another layout's style, arrangement, text or special features without permission. If you borrow ideas, get **authorization from the original author**, credit them in the description, and send proof of authorization to the review group.
3. **No commercial promotion** — no advertising of any kind.

### File requirements

1. **Naming** — name the eight-character archive appropriately and avoid prohibited words (for example "cheat", "invincible", "exclusive", "strongest").
2. **Clear classification** — fill in the category and language fields completely.
3. **Screenshots** — use screenshots that show the layout itself, not unrelated gameplay.
4. **Icons** — keep the icon close to a 1:1 aspect ratio; personal avatars are fine.
5. **Recommended qualities** — an attractive and original style, sensible placement, varied button functions, and at least two control groups.

### Common problems

- **Wrong or meaningless images** — the icon should be close to 1:1 and recognizable; screenshots should show the layout, not the game.
- **Copying** — check for copied material, credit the original author and provide authorization.
- **Wrong file type** — upload the eight-character archive; do not upload raw `.json` files, rename the archive or re-zip the JSON.
- **Personal use or low quality** — do not upload layouts that only suit your own habits or are obviously unfinished.
- **Unqualified styles** — avoid strange colors, extreme brightness or transparency, and misaligned buttons.
- **Withdrawn files** — approved and rejected files are withdrawn from the group after review to keep things organized. Watch the review notifications.
- **No response yet** — reviews are done by hand. Approval does not mean the layout is listed in the launcher immediately; please be patient.

### Group rules

The review group is strictly moderated:

- Members who do not upload a layout within one day of joining are removed.
- Multiple accounts and idle chatting are not allowed.
- Any unauthorized use of another layout's style must be reworked into something original before uploading.
